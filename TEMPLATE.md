## Context

*What was the system/situation before this event? What was the agent or
operator trying to accomplish? One to three sentences — enough for a reader
to orient without knowing your setup.*

---

## Trigger

*What caused this to happen? A specific command, a deployment, a race
condition, a misconfiguration? Be concrete. If there was a timestamp or
sequence of events, include it.*

---

## Diagnosis

*How was the root cause identified? What signals were available (logs,
alerts, agent output, operator observation)? What dead ends did the
investigation hit before finding the real cause?*

---

## Action

*What was done to address it? In what order? If multiple approaches were
tried, include the ones that did not work — they are often more instructive
than the one that did.*

---

## Verification

*How was it confirmed that the action worked? What observable evidence
(output, metrics, manual test, audit log entry) demonstrated the fix?
"I ran it and it looked fine" is not verification — name the artifact.*

---

## Generalization

*What pattern does this represent? What would you tell another practitioner
running a similar system? Keep this section transferable: strip out your
specific toolnames where the lesson generalizes beyond them.*
